## 7. Storage Cleanup & Hygiene Recommendations

Based on the inventory scan, several actionable opportunities exist to recover 15 - 25 GB of cloud storage:

1. **Duplicate Massive Textbooks:**
   - `Anatomy_and_Physiology_2e_-_WEB_c9nD9QL.pdf` (430.8 MB) and its duplicate `Anatomy_and_Physiology_2e_-_WEB_c9nD9QL (1).pdf` (430.8 MB) reside directly at root. Deleting the duplicate instantly saves **~431 MB**.
2. **Uncompressed WAV Audio Files:**
   - Under `Personal/untitled folder/mac downloads/MacOS Downloads 071424/WAV/`, multiple raw WAV versions of podcast episodes consume **~6.5 GB** (e.g. Adderall & Modafinil WAVs alone are 2.46 GB, 1.85 GB, 690 MB, and 545 MB). Converting to MP3 or deleting redundant takes will free gigabytes.
3. **Redundant macOS DMG & App Installers:**
   - Large installers like `CapCut_2_2_5-beta2.dmg` (864 MB), `OpenCore-Patcher-GUI.app.zip` (585 MB), and `RECentralExpress.dmg` (422 MB) are stored in Mac Downloads archives.
4. **Python Virtual Environments / Bytecode Caches:**
   - `AMAZON/Backup` contains **6,637 `.pyc` files** and virtual environment artifacts. Python bytecode files can safely be excluded or purged if this is an archive.
5. **OneNote Image Cache Tiles:**
   - `Notes/onenote backup` contains **17,105 individual `.png` files**. These are slice exports from OneNote notebooks. Consolidating or archiving them into a single zip file will dramatically speed up Google Drive syncing and reduce metadata overhead.
6. **Root Directory Organization:**
   - There are **400 files sitting in the root directory**. Grouping them into folders such as `Research/`, `Amazon/`, `Briefings/`, and `Archive/` will make navigation in Google Drive web and desktop significantly cleaner.
