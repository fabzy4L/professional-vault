## Selection Criteria (what actually makes a track or role "good")

Weigh any opportunity — not by prestige or evidentiary fit alone — against:

1. **Leverage supplied** — does this role hand him capital, network, domain credibility, or positioning that makes owning-his-own-thing more reachable later?
2. **Bandwidth cost** — does the day-to-day workload leave real personal time to keep building the venture, or does it consume all available hours/energy?
3. **Positioning** — does the role's title/domain/network plausibly compound toward the eventual venture (e.g., a regenerative-medicine-adjacent Director role positions better than an unrelated one at equal comp)?

A high-comp, high-prestige role that consumes all bandwidth is a *worse* fit than a moderate one that leaves room to build.
- **Compensation:** Not the top priority in isolation, but functions as fuel for the North Star — it buys runway and reduces pressure to build the venture only nights/weekends. Read comp asks as "how much leverage does this buy," not "what's the biggest number."
