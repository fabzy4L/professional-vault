**Fit against North Star (see `Career_Preferences.md`):** Strong on positioning — puts him inside the actual industry he'd eventually build a venture in, with real network/credibility value. Untested on bandwidth: Director-level ops roles can be as demanding as Amazon's, and these are almost never remote (plant-floor presence expected) — conflicts with the stated remote/Southeast preference unless comp/incentives justify relocation. Diligence workload culture and on-site requirements before treating any specific opening as a fit.

**Materials that exist:** `Fabian_Alvarez_Director_Ops_Biopharma.md/.pdf`, master resume.
