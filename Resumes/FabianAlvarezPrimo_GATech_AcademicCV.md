**SERT S438T — Computational Pharmacology of Escitalopram Binding** *(May 2026, solo, git-verified)*
Independent computational structural biology study of the S438T point mutation in the human serotonin transporter (SERT/*SLC6A4*) and its effect on escitalopram (SSRI) binding. Explicitly framed as originating from the TTUHSCEP postdoctoral iPSC/postmortem-brain transcriptomic work on major depressive disorder. Performed *in-silico* S438T mutagenesis (NERF algorithm, PDB 5I6Z) and multi-seed AutoDock Vina docking (n=5 seeds, exhaustiveness=32); found the rigid-receptor model did not reproduce the experimentally reported 320-fold affinity loss (Andersen et al., 2009) — a well-reasoned negative/nuanced result, with the discrepancy discussed against known limitations of static docking and molecular dynamics proposed as the next step. Full research article, methods, and computational pipeline documented in-repo. References a forthcoming companion review, *"Cell-Type-Resolved Transcriptomics in Mood and Psychotic Disorders"* (medRxiv, in preparation). GitHub: fabzy4L/sert-s438t-escitalopram
