## Central strategic bet: the "cell / biomanufacturing science" reframe

Georgia Tech BME hosts the **NSF ERC for Cell Manufacturing Technologies (CMaT)** and the
**Marcus Center for Therapeutic Cell Characterization & Manufacturing (MC3M)** — the national hub
for the science of *manufacturing cell & gene therapies reproducibly, at scale, under quality control.*

That field is fundamentally about **process, scale-up, quality systems, automation, and data** applied
to living therapeutics. Reframed this way, Fabian's "left academia for Amazon ops" arc becomes his
single biggest differentiator, not a liability.

| Cell-manufacturing research needs | What Fabian uniquely brings |
|---|---|
| cGMP cell-therapy process knowledge | iPSC reprogramming under cGMP (TTUHSCEP) |
| Reproducibility / quality systems | CLIA/COLA lab supervision; 99.7% quality compliance |
| Scale-up & throughput engineering | Amazon ops: 82% throughput gain, Lean, staffing models |
| Process analytics / automation / ML | Python/R/SQL pipelines, real-time dashboards, custom automation |
| Biomaterials for cell delivery/scaffolds | PhD in biomedical materials; conductive tissue composites |

**Pitch line (draft):** "Went and learned how large-scale regulated operations actually work, and is
bringing that rigor back to the hardest bottleneck in cell therapy — manufacturing."

## Hard truths to design around (do NOT paper over)

1. **Rank.** "Open rank" ≠ apply for tenured. Tenured needs an established, *funded, independently-run*
   program. Apply effectively at **tenure-track Assistant**; write everything as "the lab I will build."
2. **Funding is the real bar.** Research statement must name specific fundable aims + sources
   (NIH NIBIB, NSF, ARPA-H, GA cell-therapy initiatives, industry). Amazon/CLIA background is genuine
   evidence he can run a program on budget/timeline.
3. **Publication recency/authorship.** Committees weight recent first/senior-author, high-impact papers.
   Need the full list to calibrate. Likely a genuine weak point → counterweight is the distinctive niche.
4. **No PI track record / grants yet.** Normal at assistant level, but shifts weight onto the vision.
