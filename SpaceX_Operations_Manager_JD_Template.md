## Composite Job Description

**OPERATIONS MANAGER (STARSHIP / STARLINK)**

The Operations Manager takes *extreme ownership* of a production-support function (material flow, inspection, maintenance, facilities, or planning) that feeds the build of rockets, spacecraft, or Starlink hardware. The goal is for the function to never be the bottleneck. Hardware should flow at full rate, safely and in compliance, while the team keeps scaling.

### Responsibilities

**People leadership**
- Lead supervisors, specialists, technicians, and associates across multiple shifts and work centers
- Run reviews, 1:1 mentoring, training plans, skills matrices, scheduling, timecards, and recruiting
- Develop supervisors and grow the talent pipeline; hold the team accountable to standards

**Execution & flow**
- Own day-to-day flow (receipt, stow, pick, pack, final-mile delivery to work centers, or the inspection/maintenance equivalent) with minimal impact to line rate
- Keep the function from holding up hardware unless defects require corrective action
- Support 24/7 operations, including off-hours and emergency response

**Metrics & continuous improvement**
- Build data-driven goals, KPIs, and dashboards, and present them to cross-functional leaders
- Drive root-cause determination and corrective action (RCCA) to eliminate recurrences
- Lead lean, efficiency, and cost-saving projects in a fast-changing environment

**Safety, quality & compliance**
- Run a safe operation, targeting zero OSHA recordables
- Meet AS9100 and customer flow-down requirements, with audit-ready process control and cycle counts
- Manage shelf-life, FIFO, and environmental-control requirements (materials roles)

**Cross-functional & financial**
- Partner with Engineering, Production, Procurement, Build Reliability, and Design
- Manage CapEx and OpEx, justify equipment and upgrades, and give DFM/maintainability feedback
- Forecast, risk-manage, and balance cost, reliability, and production rate (planning roles)

### Basic Qualifications (common floor)
- **Advanced degree + 3 yrs**, OR bachelor's + 5 yrs, OR 9 yrs in: operations or supply chain management, material/production planning, purchasing, distribution, project management, master production scheduling, or material cost control
- **3+ yrs of personnel management with formal direct reports** (Materials Mgmt); 2+ yrs in a leadership position (Maintenance); 5+ yrs of leadership in a manufacturing or industrial environment

### Preferred
- Bachelor's in engineering or a technical field (materials science is named for NDE)
- Large-scale production of highly engineered products; aerospace, aviation, automotive, or other high-volume manufacturing
- Data fluency: Excel (advanced), SQL, Python, Tableau/Power BI; ERP and CMMS systems
- Communicating with senior leadership; adapting to abrupt strategy changes
- Role-specific: NDE Level II (ASNT TC-1A / NAS 410), AWS CWI, hydraulics/pneumatics/controls, capital planning

### Additional Requirements
- Extended hours and weekends; 24/7 on-call for emergencies
- On-site (Starbase roles: remote not considered); travel 10–50% depending on role
- Lift 25 lbs and work on the factory floor; ITAR (U.S. person) applies

### Compensation reference
Manager band (NDE posting): **$140,000–$180,000** base, plus equity/ESPP. Other postings omit pay.
